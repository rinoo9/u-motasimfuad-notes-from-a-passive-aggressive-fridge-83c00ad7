Entry for the make.fun bounty "Notes From a Passive-Aggressive Fridge" — one funny passive-aggressive note a smart fridge leaves for its owner.

> "I hid the chocolate behind the vegetables, where you never look, and logged your 2 a.m. visit in the family group chat. The light stays on. I saw everything."

Character count: 157.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.

Why it works: the fridge hides the chocolate in the one place the owner
never looks, then escalates a private 2 a.m. snack raid into a public
family group chat announcement. The closing callback — the light stays
on, the fridge saw everything — keeps the passive-aggressive witness
voice while the new middle turns a midnight snack into a social crime.
